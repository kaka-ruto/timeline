I saw our delivery pipeline was open to risky npm packages. So I bolted on a dependency safety guard: a minimum release-age policy in CI. Now, packages have to prove they've been around before we use them. This blocks all the freshly published, potentially dangerous code. It's a simple, cheap shield that cuts supply-chain risk. Simple guardrails in CI can prevent expensive security surprises. They're worth the effort.
